Local models on your own machine (optional)
In the pasted workflow, replace the values you want the app to fill with these tokens:
%PROMPT% — the outfit instruction text
%ANCHOR% — filename of your anchor photo
%GARMENT1%, %GARMENT2%, %GARMENT3% — garment photo filenames
Images are uploaded to ComfyUI first, then the tokens become their filenames.

Both servers need CORS enabled: OLLAMA_ORIGINS=* and ComfyUI's --enable-cors-header. Browsers block plain http from an https page, so use local models with the page opened locally.
